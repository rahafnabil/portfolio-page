My Projects

a pearl's legacy

An immersive VR experience inspired by Bahrain’s pearling heritage using Unity 6 and Meta Quest 2. Created interactive 3D environments and VR interactions with XR tools. Integrated Convai conversational AI to enhance user engagement and deployed the project as an APK.

my role

I worked on both the technical and visual development of the experience, creating and integrating 3D environments and interactive VR elements in Unity. I also wrote multiple scripts to control interactions and user behavior within the experience, helping connect the environment, objects, and VR systems together. In addition, I integrated Convai conversational AI into the project, allowing users to interact with AI-driven characters in a more natural way and strengthening the cultural storytelling aspect of the experience.

sindbad: seas of destiny

Sindbad is an Arabic-first game inspired by the legendary voyages of Sinbad, designed to bring the story to life through exploration, stylized environments, and culturally influenced world-building. Developed natively in Arabic by a team of around 200 people, the project places strong emphasis on regional storytelling and creating an experience that feels authentic to Arabic-speaking players.

my role

3D Environment Art : During my internship with The Winning Entertainment Group, I contributed to the Sindbad game by creating and optimizing 3D assets for use in the game environment. My work included modelling, UV mapping, texturing, and material baking, with attention to keeping assets visually consistent and performance-ready for integration. I also carried out historical reference research and collaborated with the environment team to support the game’s world-building and overall visual direction.
